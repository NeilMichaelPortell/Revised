  Why This Is Serious:
      Your computer is now unprotected from viruses and malware
  Message:
      Windows Defender real-time monitoring was disabled.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  📌  Windows Defender real-time protection was just turned off.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  🔍  WHAT HAPPENED
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Your Windows Defender real-time monitoring was disabled, leaving
  your computer unprotected from viruses and malware. This means
  any file you open or website you visit can be scanned by
  nothing, allowing malware to execute freely without detection.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ⚠   WHY THIS IS RISKY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  This is serious because your computer is now vulnerable to
  infections that can steal your personal data, compromise your
  online accounts, or even take control of your device. As the
  attacker's goal is often to disable Defender as their first
  step, this is a classic indicator of an active infection or a
  compromised administrator account.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ✅  HOW TO PREVENT THIS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Re-enable real-time protection immediately: Start → Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → turn on.
  • Do not download or open anything until protection is restored.
  • Check if any software 'required' you to disable it — that software is malicious.
  • If you cannot re-enable it, run the Windows Defender Offline scan from a restart.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  💡  LEARNING TIP
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Be cautious of repeated patterns like USB devices being plugged
  in multiple times. This could be an indication of malware
  activity.

  Risk score: 10  │  Source: security:defender_disabled
